How it works

Tap. Pay. Get money back at the shops you love.

  1. 1

    Tap at the counter

    A small chip sits by the register. Customers tap their phone and they're done. Nothing at the counter gets replaced, and there's nothing new for staff to learn.

  2. 2

    Every shop glows a color

    Each hour a shop shows a color, from red (1% back) to violet (up to 10%). Quiet hours glow brightest. Paying with Lightning adds one more color.

  3. 3

    Light up your map

    Every visit fills in your own map of the neighborhood, color by color, as you find the places on your street.

A Lightning payer pays the shop directly, and Lux never holds the money. The shop keeps the fee it saves and passes it on: Lightning payers see the shop one color higher. Not on Lightning? A customer's taps roll up into one bank payment a week, which also costs the shop far less than a card.

For shops

Every corner café gets its own revenue manager.

Owners told us they love flexible rewards but don't have time to run them. So Lux runs them on autopilot. It sets each hour's reward from the shop's own rhythm: low in the rush, high when it's quiet. The whole month stays within what card fees would have cost.

  • Learns. From each shop's visits and hours, plus weather, foot traffic and local events.
  • Asks. When it isn't sure, it checks with the counter: “Busy right now?” Every answer makes it smarter.
  • Owners lead. Pin the hours you already know. Autopilot fills the rest, and one tap overrides any hour.
  • Proves itself. Every statement shows what Lux cost next to what a card terminal would have charged.
